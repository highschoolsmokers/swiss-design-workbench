01

Statement

Why this site exists, and what it collects.

This is a working archive of two parallel practices: a life in writing and editing, and a life in software. They are not kept apart. The tools are built for the writing; the writing sets the problems for the tools.

Everything here — essays, projects, residencies, open source — is documented in the same register, on the same grid, with the same typeface. The form is part of the argument.

02

About

The short version, in full sentences.

W. S. Gong is a fiction editor at The Rumpus and a technical writer with a twenty-five-year career in software. He writes short fiction and essays, and builds small, literate tools at the seam between large language models and the craft of writing.

Recent work has focused on agents that assist editors without replacing them — retrieval-first pipelines, verifiable citations, and human-in-the-loop review surfaces.
